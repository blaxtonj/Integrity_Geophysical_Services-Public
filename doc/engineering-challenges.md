## Engineering Challenges

### 1. Translating Business Requirements into a Clear Web Experience

One of the primary challenges in this project was aligning technical and design decisions with the actual business goals of Integrity Geophysical Services, inc.

Unlike application-focused projects, this site functions primarily as a business-facing platform. Its purpose is to:

- communicate specialized geophysical services clearly  
- establish credibility and trust within a technical industry  
- present information in a way that is both accurate and accessible  
- support potential client inquiries

This required a shift in approach from building functionality to prioritizing clarity, structure, and communication.

As a result, several design and technical decisions were influenced by these goals:

- restructuring content to improve readability and flow  
- emphasizing clear visual hierarchy to highlight key information  
- using semantic HTML to support accessibility and clarity  
- simplifying layouts to reduce cognitive load  

This challenge reinforced the importance of designing around communication and user understanding, rather than focusing solely on implementation.

### 2. Balancing Messaging with Visual Hierarchy

A key challenge in the redesign was ensuring that the visual design supported the messaging rather than competing with it.

In the original implementation, important information was not clearly prioritized. Headings, body text, and supporting elements often carried similar visual weight, making it difficult for users to quickly identify what mattered most.

The redesign focused on establishing a clear visual hierarchy to guide user attention. This included:

- strengthening the contrast between headings and body text  
- using spacing to create clear separation between sections  
- grouping related content to improve scanability  
- reducing visual noise that distracted from key information  

These changes helped ensure that users could quickly understand the structure of the page and identify the most important content without needing to read everything in detail.

This challenge reinforced the importance of using visual design as a tool for communication, not just aesthetics.


### 3. Designing a Visual Language Aligned with Industry Expectations

A key challenge was ensuring the overall visual tone of the site aligned with the expectations of the oil and gas industry.

Unlike more expressive or marketing-driven designs, this domain requires a sense of precision, reliability, and technical credibility. Overly stylized visuals or aggressive design choices could undermine trust and make the content feel less authoritative.

The challenge was to create a modern interface while maintaining a restrained and professional aesthetic. This influenced decisions such as:

- using a controlled and minimal color palette  
- selecting typography that emphasized clarity and professionalism  
- avoiding overly decorative elements or unnecessary visual effects  
- maintaining consistency across sections to reinforce reliability  

The goal was to ensure the site felt trustworthy and aligned with industry standards, while still improving overall usability and presentation.

This challenge reinforced the importance of adapting design decisions to the expectations of the target audience and domain.
